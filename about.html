About Al Bayaan

Nurturing hearts with Quran, building a bright Islamic future.

Welcome to Al Bayaan

Quality Quran & Islamic education, from the comfort of home

Al Bayaan Quranic Sciences is an online Islamic learning platform dedicated to providing quality Quran & Islamic education for kids and adults worldwide, in a friendly, engaging, and interactive environment.

We believe learning the Quran should be easy, enjoyable, and meaningful. Our classes are designed with care to help students build confidence, improve recitation, and develop love for Islam through interactive activities and personalized learning methods.

Begin your journey with the Book of Allah from the comfort of your home — we make Qur'anic learning simple, structured, and spiritual for kids, sisters & brothers worldwide.

Our approach

Learn. Understand. Live.

Learn
