FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long is each class?

Class length depends on the course and the student's age — younger children have shorter classes so they stay focused, while older students and adults have longer sessions. We'll suggest the right length during your free trial.

What device and internet do I need?

Classes are held on Zoom, so a phone, tablet, laptop or computer with the Zoom app is all you need, along with a stable internet connection. A headset or earphones help with hearing pronunciation clearly.

How will I know how my child is progressing?

The teacher sends parents a progress update on WhatsApp every month, covering what was learned, how the student is doing, and what to practise next.

What happens if a class is missed?

If you let the teacher know in advance, the class will be rescheduled to another time that suits you.

Can I choose between a male and a female teacher?

Yes. We have qualified male and female teachers — just select your preference in the form above.
